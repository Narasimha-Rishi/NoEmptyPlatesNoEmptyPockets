Why we made this

Why we started this nonprofit

NoEmptyPlatesNoEmptyPockets is a student-run nonprofit that runs food drives to fight hunger and poverty. We started it because too many people in our community go without enough food and basic necessities, and that affects their health and their daily lives. We wanted to turn that concern into real action.

It is also our HOSA project, a way for us to put service and community health into practice. Our goal is 3,000 pounds of food and 300 care packages in 30 days.

Host your own drive

Start a drive in five steps

Anyone can run a food drive. Here is a simple way to begin.

  1. Pick a place and a date. A school, club, store or neighborhood all work. Ask permission from whoever runs the space.
  2. Set a goal. Decide how many pounds of food or care packages you want, and how long the drive will run.
  3. Spread the word. Share the date, the place and the list of items to bring.
  4. Collect and count. Set out a box, sort what comes in, and keep a count of pounds and packages.
  5. Contact us. Reach out to our team so we can help coordinate getting your donations to the Northshore Food Pantry.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What can I donate?

Unopened, non-expired, non-perishable food such as canned vegetables, beans and soup, rice, pasta, peanut butter, and cereal. For care packages, we welcome toothbrushes, toothpaste, travel-size soap and shampoo, warm socks, and granola bars.

Where do I drop off a donation?

Our next drive is Saturday, November 21 at Apna Bazar, 20710 Bothell Everett Hwy, Bothell, WA 98012. For other drop-off options, contact the team.

Where does the food go?

Everything goes to the Northshore Food Pantry, which gives it to Northshore schools and to homeless shelters and food banks in the Bothell area.

Do I have to be a student to help?

No. Anyone can donate, volunteer or host a drive.

How can I host a drive?

Follow the five steps above, then contact the team so we can help.
